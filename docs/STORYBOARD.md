# The Generations of Adam (Genesis 5): storyboard

Ultra tier, 1080p60, every frame drawn in code. One idea carries the whole song: the generations are a
line of standing stones up a long valley, each with a clay lamp at its foot, and the chorus is the
flame passed lamp to lamp in a rock-cut hall. Every lyric is cut into something in the world (stone,
rock, clay, wood, dust) and fills with light as it is sung. No people are shown; Enoch's walk is
footprints made by no one seen.
