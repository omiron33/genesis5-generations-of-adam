When the earth was new to man,
and the image of God was in his hands...
Adam begot a son in his likeness,
after his own form, under the garden's shadow.
And he called his name Seth,
for God had set him down where the wound could grow.
And the days went on like weather,
and sons and daughters followed after.
The generations of Adam
go on, go on, go on
The generations of Adam
and the dust keeps singing on
In sons and daughters
the line goes on
Seth lived and begot Enos,
then men began to call on the Name.
Enos begot Cainan,
and Cainan begot Maleleel,
and the years were long as lamp smoke
over the homes of the earth.
Sons and daughters, sons and daughters,
one after one, the name survives.
The generations of Adam
go on, go on, go on
The generations of Adam
and the dust keeps singing on
In sons and daughters

Maleleel begot Jared,
and Jared begot Enoch.
Then came the old refrain,
the house of man, the count of days.
Mathusala rose from Enoch,
and Lamech from Mathusala,
and still the ground held its burden
beneath every foot that walked.
Death in the doorway,
death at the gate,
every cradle leaning
toward the same old wait...
death in the doorway,
death in the shade,
until one walked different
and the silence broke its chain
The generations of Adam
go on, go on, go on
The generations of Adam

In sons and daughters

Enoch walked with God,
and he pleased Him in the path.
No darkness took him down,
no grave laid claim to his breath.
He walked with God,
and God took him.
And heaven opened like a door
for a man who stayed so near.
Then back to earth, the names remain,
the lamps still burn, the hands still ache.
Mathusala, Lamech, the old road wide,
the promise moving under the weight.
Lamech looked on the cursed ground,
on the sweat and thorns and the wearied face,
and spoke of rest through trembling breath:
“From our toil, this one will bring us ease.”
